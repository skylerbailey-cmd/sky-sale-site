Terms

The deal, in plain words.

SkySale is a point-of-sale and CRM for retail shops, operated by Skyler Bailey Consulting. Using it means agreeing to what follows. Last updated 25 September 2026.

What we provide

Software for ringing up sales, working out commission and payroll, keeping a customer list, sending those customers email, booking sessions, and checking what a card processor actually settled. We do not take payments ourselves: a shop brings its own processor and its own merchant account.

Your account

A shop is responsible for who it lets in. Staff PINs unlock the money screens — payroll, settings, the transaction list — so they should be treated as credentials rather than convenience codes. Tell us at security@sky-sale.com if you think somebody has access they should not.

Your data is yours

Your sales, your customers and your staff records belong to you. We hold them to run the service and for nothing else — see the privacy page. You can export them at any time, and closing your account deletes them immediately and permanently.

Email you send through us

A shop writes to its own customers — people who bought from it. It must not use SkySale to send unsolicited mail to a list it did not build, and it must honour a request to stop. We will suspend an account that does otherwise, because mail sent from here affects every other shop's delivery.

What we promise, and what we do not

We work to keep the service available and correct, and we fix what we find. We do not promise it will never be unavailable, and it is provided as it is. Your figures are yours to check: reconcile your own statements, and tell us promptly if something looks wrong so we can look at it while it is fresh.

Ending it

You can close your account whenever you like, from Settings. We can end an account that is being used to break these terms or the law, and we will say why and give you a chance to export first unless the law prevents us.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we will say so by email to the address your shop signs in with, before it takes effect.

Getting hold of us

hello@sky-sale.com for anything, security@sky-sale.com for security, privacy@sky-sale.com for your data.

Questions
