6. Plans, fees and billing

Full plan

SkySale costs $115 per store location per month, plus any applicable sales tax, VAT or similar taxes, which are added to the price. New subscriptions include a 14-day free trial. A payment method is collected when you start, and the first charge is made when the trial ends unless you cancel before then.

Sleep plan

A shop that is closed for a while can be put to sleep for $15 per store location per month. While asleep, everything already recorded is kept and stays readable and exportable, but the Service will not take sales, returns or exchanges, will not book or move appointments, and will not send email. Appointments already booked can still be cancelled. When you move from the full plan to sleep, the unused part of the current period at the full price is credited against future invoices. When you wake a sleeping shop, it returns to the full plan and the remainder of the current period at the full price is charged straight away; the shop wakes once that payment succeeds.

Billing

Fees are billed monthly in advance through our payment provider, Stripe, and renew automatically each month until cancelled. You authorise us, through Stripe, to charge your payment method for all fees and taxes due. We may change prices on at least 30 days' notice by email to the address your shop signs in with. A change takes effect from your next billing period after the notice period ends; if you do not accept it, you can cancel before it takes effect.

7. Cancelling, and what happens to your data

A manager or admin can cancel the subscription at any time in Settings → Account. The shop keeps working until the end of the period already paid for (or the end of the trial). After that, the Service stops taking new sales and your records remain readable for a short time.

Thirty (30) days after the subscription ends, the shop's account and everything in it are permanently deleted — sales, receipts, returns and chargebacks, customers and their purchase and email history, staff, commission and payroll records, products, prices, treatments and bookings, and the subscription record. We will email the address your shop signs in with at least seven (7) days before deletion, and deletion will not happen until at least seven days after that email is sent. To keep your records, start the subscription again or put the shop to sleep before then. Export anything you want to keep first; once deleted, we cannot restore it.

An admin can instead close the account immediately in Settings → Account. Closing ends the subscription and permanently deletes the account and everything in it at once, with no grace period.
