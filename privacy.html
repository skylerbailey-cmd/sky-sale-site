Privacy Policy

What we hold, and what we do with it.

How GRND Labs LLC handles information in SkySale — what a shop records, who else touches it, and when it is deleted.

Effective 1 October 2026 · last updated 8 November 2026

In short

  • GRND Labs LLC runs SkySale. We keep what a shop records — its sales, staff and customers — to run SkySale for that shop, and nothing else.
  • For a shop's customers and staff, the shop is in charge and we act for it.
  • We never see full card numbers, never sell data, show no ads, and this website has no trackers.
  • Some features use AI from Anthropic, including our WhatsApp and email support, which looks up what it needs in the shop's records to answer. Anthropic isn't allowed to train on what we send.
  • If a shop connects another app or AI tool to SkySale, we send that app what the shop allows it to read, and an owner or manager may let their AI assistant change the shop's setup. The shop chooses the app.
  • A cancelled shop's data is deleted 30 days after its subscription ends, with an email a week before. Closing the account deletes it at once.
  • Wherever you live, you can ask for a copy of your information, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it's used. In the EEA and UK these are your rights under the GDPR, and you can complain to your data protection authority. A shop can download or delete a customer's data from that customer's details.

This summary is here to help; the full text below is what applies.

1. Who we are

SkySale is provided by GRND Labs LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("GRND Labs", "we", "us"). This policy covers the SkySale app and service, any SkySale mobile app, and this website. Questions about it go to privacy@sky-sale.com, and a person will answer.

2. Our role: two kinds of information

About shops. For the business that holds a SkySale account and the people who run it, we decide how the information is used, and this policy applies directly.

About a shop's customers and staff. Customer and staff records are collected by the shop, and we hold and process them on the shop's behalf and on its instructions. The shop decides what to collect and is responsible for telling its customers and staff how it uses their information. If you are a customer of a shop that uses SkySale, that shop is the right place to start with a question or request about your information; we will help the shop act on it.

3. What we collect

From a shop, when it signs up and uses SkySale

  • Account and store details: the email address the shop signs in with, store and company name, address, phone, store email, time zone, tax rate, receipt footer, return policy and pay schedule.
  • Staff: each employee's name, PIN, role, commission terms and pay records.
  • Sales: what was sold or returned, when, by whom, prices, tax, how it was paid, and the last four digits of a card. We never receive or store full card numbers.
  • Photos on a sale: pictures staff take or upload on a sale's receipt, such as the product as it left or a signed form. They are stored with that sale in our database, not left on the device, so the shop can see them again from any register. SkySale uses a device's camera only when someone taps Take Photo, and the device asks permission first.
  • Products, treatments, availability and bookings.
  • Billing: your subscription plan and status. Card details for your SkySale subscription are entered on Stripe's page and held by Stripe, not by us.
  • Connections you choose to make: tokens for your Gmail account, and the keys for your payment processor or gateway (Maverick, Payarc, Authorize.Net, NMI — including gateways built on it, such as Easy Pay Direct's — or USAePay), and the settlement and chargeback records we read with them, which can include the last four digits of a card.
  • The SkySale app: if you turn on notifications, the push token Apple issues for that device and which notifications you chose. It is used only to send those notifications, and is deleted when Apple tells us it is no longer valid or the account is closed.
  • Connections a shop sets up: the name and permissions of each API key (we keep only a one-way fingerprint of the key itself, never the key), the web addresses its webhooks send to, their signing secrets (encrypted), and a log of each webhook delivery, including a copy of what was sent. For an AI assistant an owner or manager connects by signing in: the assistant's name and the address it returns to, who connected it and the email address they used, what they allowed it to do, one-way fingerprints of its access tokens (never the tokens), and a record of each change it made.
  • What you send us: questions asked in the help chat, feature requests (with the name you give and your account's email address), and emails to support.
  • Support on WhatsApp: your WhatsApp number and profile name, your messages and our replies, and — if you link the number to your SkySale profile — which shop and employee record it belongs to. Changes you ask support to make, and whether you confirmed them, are recorded.
  • Problems you report: when you tell support something isn't working, we write it up as a report for our engineers, with your shop's name but without your contact details or your customers' details, and keep your email address (or WhatsApp number) so we can tell you when it's fixed.

About a shop's customers, recorded by the shop

This information belongs to the shop. The shop decides what to record and how to use it; we hold and process it on the shop's behalf, as its service provider, and only to run SkySale for it. If you are a customer of a shop, ask that shop about your information — and we'll help them answer.

  • Name, email address, phone number, and, if the shop records them, address, birthday and notes. Notes are free text written by the shop's staff; we ask shops not to use them for health or other sensitive information unless they need it and have the customer's consent.
  • What they bought or returned and when, the employees who served them, bookings, and the emails the shop sent them.
  • Photos the shop's staff attach to a sale, which may show the customer. Only someone signed in to that shop can see them, and only a manager can remove one. We ask shops to photograph a customer only with their consent, and not to use photos for health or other sensitive information unless they need it and have the customer's consent.
  • If the shop uses them, the customer's signature on the shop's own waiver (with their name and the waiver's exact words as they read it) and on their receipt, drawn on the shop's screen. These are kept with the sale as the shop's record of what was agreed, and only someone signed in to that shop can see them. The waiver's wording is the shop's, not ours.

About businesses we contact

We may email salons, spas and shops in the United States that we think could use SkySale. We find them from public sources — business listings such as Google Maps, and their own websites — and keep the business's name, location and website, and the name, role and work email address of the person we write to. Every email says how to stop them, and anyone who asks is never emailed again; we send at most three emails in six months to someone who hasn't replied. We don't do this outside the United States.

Automatically

  • Cookies and similar storage that keep you signed in (a session cookie lasting up to 30 days, and a signed cookie that remembers which shop you signed in to for up to a year, until you sign out) and remember preferences such as the company you last used. We use no advertising or analytics cookies, and this website loads no trackers.
  • Server logs kept by our hosting providers — such as IP address, browser and the pages requested — used for security and troubleshooting.

4. How we use it

  • To provide SkySale: ring up and record sales, work out commission and payroll, keep customer records, send the emails the shop asks us to send, take bookings and reconcile against the shop's processor.
  • To sign you in, bill the subscription, and send service messages — sign-in links, billing notices, and warnings such as the notice before a cancelled shop's data is deleted.
  • To answer help questions and support requests, and to consider feature requests.
  • To keep SkySale secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and improve how it works.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, we do not show ads, and we do not use one shop's data to serve another.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, we rely on these legal bases for the information we decide about (Section 2). For customer and staff records we hold for a shop, the shop decides the legal basis.

  • Performing our contract with the shop: providing SkySale, signing people in, billing, service messages and support.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping SkySale secure and preventing abuse, fixing and improving it, answering support requests from people who aren't account holders, and contacting businesses that could use SkySale (Section 3). You can object to any of these (Section 11).
  • Legal obligations: tax and accounting records, and answering lawful requests.
  • Consent: push notifications and the camera, which you can turn off at any time in your device's settings.

We don't make decisions about people by automated means alone that have legal or similarly significant effects on them. Commission and payroll figures are calculations the shop checks and decides on.

6. AI features

Some features send information to Anthropic, whose Claude models generate text for us:

  • Help chat: the questions you type and the conversation so far.
  • Product set-up: the text of a supplier web page you ask us to read, and a product's name, brand and description, to write descriptions and usage notes.
  • Welcome email review: the shop's own products' names, brands, descriptions, benefits and usage notes, to suggest clearer wording.
  • Support by WhatsApp and email: your messages, the conversation so far, and what the assistant looks up to answer you. For someone linked to a shop, that can include the shop's settings, products, sales and returns, bookings, disputes, a customer's record when you ask about them, and pay or commission figures — your own, or the shop's for an owner or manager. Before an email reply is sent, a second AI check reads it, and a person reads any reply it isn't sure of. Nothing is changed in a shop until someone with permission confirms it.

We send only what the feature needs, and never photos attached to a sale. Anthropic processes it to return a response to us under its commercial terms, which do not permit it to train its models on this data. We do not use your data to train AI models.

7. Gmail and Google user data

A shop can connect its own Gmail account so customer emails come from its own address. If it does, SkySale asks Google for permission to see the account's email address and basic profile, and to send email from that mailbox. We use it only to send the emails the shop asks us to send — receipts, welcome emails, mass email, booking messages and staff alerts. SkySale does not read, search or organise the mailbox.

SkySale's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Gmail data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not allow humans to read it except with the shop's permission, for security or abuse investigations, or where the law requires. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models, and it is never sent to the AI features described above. A shop can disconnect Gmail at any time from its Google account settings; closing the SkySale account deletes the tokens we hold.

Apps a shop connects

A shop can connect other apps to SkySale: AI assistants such as Claude or ChatGPT, and automation tools such as n8n, Zapier or Make. They can do this with an API key, a connection for AI assistants (MCP), or webhooks that send each new sale, customer or appointment to an address the shop gives us. When they do, we send that app the information the shop has allowed it to read, which can include sales, returns, products, appointments and customers' names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, birthdays, notes and purchase history. These apps are chosen by the shop, not by us, and handle the information under their own terms. They are not our service providers. API keys and webhooks can only read. An owner or manager can also connect their own AI assistant by signing in with their email, name and PIN; if they allow it, the assistant can change the shop's setup as that person — store settings, staff, products and prices, opening hours and closed days — and we send it what it needs to do that, including staff names, roles, email addresses and mobile numbers. No connection can ring up, change or refund a sale, or reach payroll, pay, commission or PINs. They never receive card numbers, not even the last four digits, or photos attached to a sale. Only the shop's owner, with an admin name and PIN, can make API keys and webhooks; the owner sees every assistant connected and every change it made, and can turn any connection off at any time.

9. How long we keep it

  • While the account is active — including while a shop is asleep — we keep its information so it can use SkySale.
  • After cancelling: the shop keeps working to the end of the period it paid for. Thirty (30) days after the subscription ends, the account and everything in it are deleted, including its customers' and staff's records. We email the shop at least seven days before, and never delete sooner than seven days after that email.
  • Closing the account in Settings → Account deletes it and everything in it immediately.
  • Webhook delivery logs are deleted after 30 days. A shop's API keys, webhooks and their logs are deleted when the account is.
  • Signatures on a sale's waiver or receipt are kept with the sale, can't be changed or removed by the shop's staff, and are deleted when the sale is deleted or the account is.
  • Photos on a sale are kept with the sale. They are deleted when a manager removes one, when the sale is deleted, or when the account is.
  • Once deleted, information is removed from our database and cannot be restored by us. Stripe keeps billing records as the law requires, and hosting providers' logs expire on their own schedules.
  • A customer's details can be deleted by the shop at any time, from that customer in Transactions → Customers. Their record, notes, the emails sent to them, and the photos and signatures on their sales are deleted; the sales themselves stay in the shop's books, without their name, email, phone or address, because businesses must keep sales records for tax.
  • WhatsApp support conversations are deleted after 30 days, and the record of changes made through WhatsApp after a year.
  • Support emails: our record of each email we handled is deleted after 12 months. Who reported a problem is kept until they've been told it's fixed and 30 days more, and no longer than 12 months. The emails themselves stay in our support mailbox; ask us and we'll delete yours.
  • Businesses we contact: kept until they ask us to stop or we no longer expect to contact them. Someone who asks us to stop is kept on a do-not-contact list, so we never email them again; ask us to delete the rest and we will.
  • Help chat questions are not stored with your account; feature requests are kept in the email inbox they are sent to.

10. Security

Signing in uses a one-time link sent to the shop's own address, so there is no password to reuse or leak. Money screens are behind each employee's PIN, and sensitive actions need a manager's code. Traffic is encrypted in transit. Mailbox (Gmail) tokens and card-processor and gateway keys are encrypted at rest, with a key kept separately from the database, and are never shown back. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a problem, write to security@sky-sale.com. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires: a shop without undue delay, so it can meet its own duties, and where we decide how the information is used, the data protection authority within 72 hours where the GDPR requires it.

11. Your choices and rights

Anyone can ask us to:

  • see the personal information we hold about them, and get a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • restrict how we use it while a question about it is settled;
  • have a copy in a format a computer can read, to take elsewhere;
  • object to how we use it on the basis of our legitimate interests, and to being contacted about SkySale at all — we will stop;
  • withdraw consent they gave, without affecting what was done before.

Write to privacy@sky-sale.com. We answer within one month (we may take two more months for complex requests, and will tell you if so), free of charge. We may need to confirm who you are first. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and we will honour them as that law requires.

If your information is held for a shop — you're its customer or employee — the shop decides about it, so start with the shop; if you write to us, we will pass your request to the shop and help it respond. A shop can do this itself: from a customer's details in Transactions → Customers, a manager can download everything held about that customer, or delete their details. A shop can see, correct and export its own records in the app at any time, and delete them by closing the account.

Complaints. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you can complain to your data protection authority — in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr) — though we'd like the chance to put things right first.

12. Children

SkySale is for businesses and is not directed to children. Shop accounts must be held by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Where information is kept

SkySale is operated from the United States, and information is stored and processed in the United States. When personal information comes from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we protect the transfer with the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK and Swiss additions), which are part of the data processing agreement we sign with shops on request. Our service providers transfer it under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where they are certified to it, or under the Standard Contractual Clauses. Write to privacy@sky-sale.com for a copy.

14. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here and update the date at the top. If a change is material, we will also tell shops before it takes effect — with a notice in SkySale, by email to the address the shop signs in with, or both.
