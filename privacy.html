Privacy

What we hold, and what we do with it.

SkySale is a point-of-sale and CRM used by retail shops. This page says plainly what it stores, who else touches it, and how to have it deleted. Last updated 25 September 2026.

Who we are

SkySale is operated by Skyler Bailey Consulting. Write to privacy@sky-sale.com about anything on this page and a person will answer.

What a shop gives us

A shop signs in with its own email address and tells us its name, address, opening hours, tax rate and pay schedule. It adds its staff — their names, the PIN each uses at the till, their role and their commission terms. It adds the products it sells, and it rings up sales.

What a shop's customers give us

Whatever the shop records at the till: a name, an email address, a phone number, what they bought and when, and any appointments they book. A customer's relationship is with the shop, not with us — we hold this on the shop's behalf, and the shop decides what to collect.

Card details

We never see or store a card number. A card is taken by the shop's own payment processor; what reaches us is the last four digits, the amount, and whether it settled. Reconciliation compares those figures against the processor's own record.

Who else touches it

  • Railway hosts the application and its database.
  • Resend delivers sign-in links and other mail we send.
  • Google, only if a shop connects its own Gmail so customer emails come from its own address. We hold a token for that mailbox and nothing else.
  • Anthropic, only when a shop asks us to read a supplier's public catalogue page to set up its products. The page's text is sent; no customer data is.
  • Maverick or Payarc, only if a shop connects its own merchant account for reconciliation.

We do not sell data, and we do not use one shop's data to serve another.

One shop never sees another

Every row belongs to exactly one company and is read only by that company's session. A shop can be given access to a second shop's figures — a manager running one payday across two locations — only by proving a manager's name and PIN at that second shop.

How long we keep it

For as long as the shop has an account. Settings → Account → Close this account deletes the shop and everything in it immediately — sales, customers, staff, payroll, products and bookings. There is no backup to restore from and no grace period, which is the point: a shop closing its account usually wants its customer list off someone else's server.

Getting a copy, or getting it deleted

A shop can export its transactions and customers from the app at any time. For anything else — including a request from a customer of one of our shops — write to privacy@sky-sale.com. If you are a customer of a shop that uses SkySale, that shop holds your record and is the right place to start; we will help them act on it.

Security

Signing in is by a one-time link sent to the shop's own address; there is no password to reuse or leak. Traffic is encrypted in transit. Payment and mailbox tokens are stored server-side and never shown back. To report a security problem, write to security@sky-sale.com.
