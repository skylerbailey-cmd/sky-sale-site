The hard parts

The rules that cost you money when software ignores them.

Not settings to configure. This is how it already behaves, because each one was a real mistake somebody paid for first.

A return counts against the day the item sold
Not the day the money went back. An August sale refunded in September belongs to August, so it comes off the commission it actually paid.
A chargeback is held, not lost
Money in dispute is withheld from the open cheque and remembered, so it can't be taken twice. Win it and it's released; lose it and it stays gone.
A negative cheque carries forward
A cheque below zero pays nothing, and the shortfall moves to the very next payday as one carried line — not by repeating every deduction behind it.
One person, one paycheck, across every location
Staff who work at more than one shop have a record at each and a single cheque, judged on the combined total.
The day ends when your shop closes
Not at midnight on a server in another timezone. Every report and pay period is read on your own clock, so the evening's sales land in the day you rang them up.
